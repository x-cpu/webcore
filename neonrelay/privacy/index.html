Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

This policy explains how NeonRelay, the IPTV player for Fire TV and Android TV published by x-cpu ("we"), handles your information. In short: we do not collect, receive or sell any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information you enter

To use NeonRelay you enter details for a TV service you already subscribe to: a server address, username and password (Xtream Codes), or a playlist link (M3U), plus an optional TV guide link. NeonRelay stores these only on your device, in the app's private storage, so it can connect to your service.

  • They are never sent to x-cpu or to anyone other than the provider you entered.
  • They are excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers.
  • Uninstalling NeonRelay, or clearing its data, deletes them.

Data stored on your device

To work, NeonRelay keeps the following on your device only: your channel list and TV guide, favorites, recently watched channels, playback positions for movies and episodes, and your settings. None of it leaves your device.

Network connections

NeonRelay connects only to:

  • the server or playlist link you entered, to load channels, the guide, movies and series and to stream video;
  • addresses listed inside your playlist or guide, such as channel logo images and stream links.

Those services are run by your provider, not by us. Their own privacy policies apply to the information they receive, such as your IP address and login.

VPN check

If you turn on Require VPN, NeonRelay asks Android whether a VPN is currently active, and it checks which known VPN apps are installed so it can offer to open one. This happens entirely on your device. NeonRelay is not a VPN and does not see or route your traffic.

What we don't do

  • No accounts with x-cpu, no sign-up.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No collection, sharing or sale of personal information.

Children

NeonRelay is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.

Contact
