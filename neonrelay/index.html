Project

NeonRelay

An IPTV player for Amazon Fire TV and Android TV / Google TV, built for the remote. Bring your own provider and get live TV, a full TV guide and on-demand movies and series in a fast neon interface.

Download free

Features

Your provider, your login

Sign in with an Xtream Codes account or paste an M3U / M3U8 playlist link. Add an XMLTV guide link, or use the guide your playlist already includes.

TV guide & catch-up

A timeline guide with now/next, and catch-up playback when your provider supports it.

Movies & series

Poster grids, resume where you left off and auto-play of the next episode.

Multi-view

Two or four live channels at once. NeonRelay respects your plan's stream limit and only plays as many screens as your provider allows.

Stream recovery

Reconnects automatically when a server drops a live stream, and unfreezes stalled video on its own.

Optional VPN lock

Turn on Require VPN and NeonRelay stops all streaming whenever your VPN disconnects.

NeonRelay does not include any channels or content. It plays streams from a service you already subscribe to. Only use it with providers and content you have the rights to watch.

Setup

Fire TV

  1. Turn on Developer options: go to Settings › My Fire TV › About and press OK on your device's name 7 times.
  2. Open the Appstore, search for Downloader (by AFTVnews) and install it.
  3. Go to Settings › My Fire TV › Developer options › Install unknown apps and turn Downloader on.
  4. Open Downloader, enter x-cpu.com/neonrelay/neonrelay.apk and select Go.
  5. Select Install, then Done. Downloader offers to delete the file to free up space; it's safe to delete.

Google TV / Android TV

  1. Turn on Developer options: go to Settings › System › About and press OK on Android TV OS build 7 times.
  2. Open the Play Store, search for Downloader (by AFTVnews) and install it.
  3. Go to Settings › Apps › Security & restrictions › Unknown sources and turn Downloader on.
  4. Open Downloader, enter x-cpu.com/neonrelay/neonrelay.apk and select Go.
  5. Select Install, then Done.

Menu names vary a little between TV brands.

FAQ

Does NeonRelay come with channels?

No. NeonRelay is a player only. You need a subscription from a TV service provider that gives you an Xtream Codes login or an M3U playlist link.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases.

Is my login safe?

Your login is stored only on your TV and is sent only to your provider, never to x-cpu. NeonRelay has no analytics or tracking. See the privacy policy.

Why can't I install it on my new Fire TV Stick?

Amazon's newest sticks (Fire TV Stick 4K Select, and the 2026 Fire TV Stick HD and Fire TV Stick 4K) run Vega OS, which can only install apps from the Amazon Appstore. NeonRelay works on Fire TV devices that run Fire OS, which includes every Fire TV Stick 4K Max, Fire TV Cube and Fire TV smart TV.

How do I update?

Repeat the download step with the same address. Installing the new version over the old one keeps your login, favorites and history.

Why does the icon look small on Fire TV?

Fire TV shows apps installed from outside the Amazon Appstore as a small icon in a grey tile. It's a Fire TV limitation and doesn't affect the app.

Video stops or won't play. What can I try?

NeonRelay retries dropped streams on its own. If a channel still won't play, check it on another device first: most playback errors come from the provider or the stream itself. If you turned on Require VPN, make sure your VPN is connected.
