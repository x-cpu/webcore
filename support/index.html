Common install problems

Downloader says "Install blocked" or nothing happens after the download.

The setting that lets Downloader install apps is off. On Fire TV, go to Settings › My Fire TV › Developer options › Install unknown apps and turn on Downloader.

I can't find Developer options on my Fire TV.

Go to Settings › My Fire TV › About and press OK on your device's name 7 times. Developer options then appears in the My Fire TV menu.

My Fire TV Stick has no Developer options at all.

The newest Fire TV Sticks run Amazon's Vega OS, which can't install apps from outside the Amazon Appstore. NeonRelay and x-Cam need a Fire TV that runs Fire OS.

"App not installed" when updating.

The version you have may have been signed differently, for example a test build. Uninstall the app, then install the new version. You'll need to enter your login again.

Contact

Email info@x-cpu.com. Include these details and we can usually answer in one reply:

  • The project and its version, shown in the app's Settings or on the download page.
  • Your device, for example "Fire TV Stick 4K Max" or "MacBook Air M2, 16 GB".
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A photo of the screen helps.

Never send passwords, provider logins or playlist links. We'll never ask for them.

x-cpu is a small studio, so replies can take a few days.
