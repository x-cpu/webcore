Project

x-Cam

Watch your Reolink cameras on Amazon Fire TV and Android TV / Google TV. x-Cam talks directly to your NVR, Home Hub or camera over your home network. There's no cloud and no Reolink account sign-in on the TV.

Download free Setup guide

New: mini player. Keep an eye on your cameras while you watch TV. x-Cam floats a small live camera window over Netflix, YouTube and your other apps, and rotates through your cameras every 30 seconds.

Features

Mini player over your shows

A small live camera window floats over Netflix and other apps while you watch, cycling through your cameras every 30 seconds. See who's at the door without leaving your show.

Up to four cameras at once

Switch between 1, 2, 3 and 4 camera layouts. Tiled layouts use each camera's lighter substream so a Fire TV Stick keeps up, and fullscreen switches to full resolution.

Recording timeline

A 24-hour timeline for each camera with recordings marked along it. Scrub in 15-minute steps and jump between clips with the remote.

Local only

Video goes straight from your NVR to your TV. Your camera login is stored encrypted on the TV and sent nowhere else.

Stays awake

Fire TV starts its screensaver over live video when you don't touch the remote. Choose how long x-Cam keeps the screen on: 30 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours or never sleep.

Built for the remote

Left and right switch cameras, OK goes fullscreen, and MENU opens the layout picker.

Dark and light themes

Or follow your TV's system setting.

Setup

Install on Fire TV

  1. Turn on Developer options: go to Settings › My Fire TV › About and press OK on your device's name 7 times.
  2. Install Downloader (by AFTVnews) from the Appstore.
  3. Go to Settings › My Fire TV › Developer options › Install unknown apps and turn Downloader on.
  4. Open Downloader, enter x-cpu.com/xcam and select Go.
  5. Select Install, then Done.

On Google TV / Android TV, the steps are the same as in the NeonRelay guide.

Connect your cameras

  1. Find your NVR or Home Hub's IP address in the Reolink app under Device settings › Network. Reserving that address in your router stops it from changing.
  2. In the Reolink app, make sure HTTP and RTSP are turned on under Network › Advanced › Server settings. Newer firmware turns them off by default.
  3. Open x-Cam and add a device. Enter the IP address and the admin username and password you use in the Reolink app.
  4. Leave the ports at 80 (HTTP) and 554 (RTSP) unless you've changed them.
  5. Select Test connection, then Save. x-Cam finds every camera behind your NVR.

A single camera without an NVR works the same way.

FAQ

Which cameras work?

Reolink NVRs, Home Hubs and standalone Reolink cameras that you can reach on your home network. Some battery cameras only stream through Reolink's own app. If a camera shows no picture, check that RTSP is turned on for it.

Can I watch from outside my home?

Not directly. x-Cam is meant for your home network. If you use a VPN into your home network, it works over that.

Test connection fails.

Check the IP address and password first. Some Reolink devices only answer over HTTPS: turn on Use HTTPS and set the HTTP port to 443.

The timeline shows recordings, but they won't play.

Some NVR firmware doesn't share recording file names, which playback needs. When x-Cam detects this, it says so on the playback screen. Live view still works. Updating the NVR firmware may fix it.

Video shows green blocks or freezes.

That's usually a weak Wi-Fi signal to the TV. Move the router closer, use the 5 GHz band, or use a wired Ethernet adapter. Fewer camera tiles at once also helps.

Why can't I install it on my new Fire TV Stick?

Amazon's newest Fire TV sticks run Vega OS and can only install Appstore apps. See the device list on the NeonRelay page.
